06

Positionierung für Firmen & Verbände

Stärken (der Hebel)

  • Daten + Praxis: ETH-Naturwissenschaft mit Mess- und Auswertungsdisziplin (R, Zeitreihen-Daten) plus jahrelange Bewegungspraxis.
  • Angewandter Nachweis: Lehrdiplom Sport (2026), J+S, Snowboard-/Trampolin-Instruktorin (Gruppen bis zehn), Sport-Assistenz ETH mit Technikbeurteilung.
  • Schnelle Einarbeitung: Imaging-/Analysepipeline der Masterarbeit weitgehend selbst erarbeitet.

Lücken (ehrlich, überbrückbar)

  • Leistungsdiagnostik (VO2/Spiroergometrie, Laktat, NIRS) noch nicht praktisch → über Mess-/Datenerfahrung positionieren; Praktikum (BASPO/EHSM) schliesst die Lücke.
  • Branchenerfahrung: früh im Berufsweg → Einstieg über Praktikum/Assistenz/Verbandsstelle statt Senior-Rollen.
  • Programmierung: R sitzt; Python/MATLAB ehrlich als „in Einarbeitung".

I am writing even though your Specialist – Sports Science posting has just closed. If the search is still open, or reopens, I would like to be considered — my background sits at an unusual angle to the role.

My CV holds two things that rarely sit together: a research master built on quantitative experiments (MSc Health Sciences and Technology, ETH Zurich, 2021–2023), and years spent coaching people how to move. In the lab I designed experiments, collected time-series data, analysed it in R, and turned the results into reports. Alongside, I teach snowboarding and trampoline to groups of up to ten and completed the practical side of first-year sports teaching at ETH; in 2026 I finish the ETH teaching diploma in physical education.

I want to be straight about the gap: I have not yet used the physiological testing stack you list (metabolic carts/VO2, portable gas analysers, blood lactate, NIRS), and my main analysis language is R rather than Python or MATLAB. What I can say is that I pick up new instruments and toolchains fast. Given a testing protocol, I would expect to be useful within weeks.

I would gladly send my CV and thesis, and talk about how I could contribute.
